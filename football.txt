🏏 The Grandeur of the Willow and the Leather
I. The Hallowed Ground
Upon the hallowed ground, the sun descends, Where greenest turf with boundary rope extends. A silence falls, though thousands hold their breath, A stage prepared for victory or death— Not of the body, but of pride and nerve, As ancient rivalries the senses serve. From Lord's white canvas to the MCG's roar, A simple game we cherish and adore. The white flannels, a testament to time, A sport of patience, strategy, and rhyme. The umpire stands, the keepers take their place, A slow, deliberate, majestic pace. The crimson sphere, polished and hard and true, A duel starts anew, forever new. The crowds are hushed, then burst in sudden sound, As the first ball is bowled upon the ground.

II. The Battle of the Bat
The batsman steps, the willow feels the light, A sentinel awaiting day and night. He reads the seam, the subtle wristy turn, The thousand tiny lessons he must learn. A fierce straight drive, the leather screams and flies, A boundary reached, beneath approving skies. The square cut flashes, elegant and swift, A pure connection, a magnificent gift. He drops and runs, a sudden burst of speed, Two athletes meeting, planting the required seed. The long Test innings, a marathon of will, Defiance etched upon the distant hill. He builds the score, block by block, brick by brick, Ignoring the slow, the fast, the subtle trick. The century stands, a monumental score, The moment savoured, asked for nothing more. But caution waits, for one mistake will call The dreaded silence, the impending fall.

III. The Art of the Ball
The bowler glides, a poetry in motion, A mix of thunder and precise devotion. The fast man runs, the muscles tight and strained, A lightning strike, a wicket to be gained. The yorker drills, a perfect, deadly dart, Breaking the guard, shattering the heart. The spinner's magic, flight and dip and drift, A calculated, slow, and poisoned gift. The ball hangs high, then turns upon the pitch, Leaving the batsman searching in a ditch. The appeal goes up, a collective, soaring cry, The finger raised, confirming fate's reply. The hat-trick chance, a tension sharp and keen, The rarest jewel on this expansive scene. The fielding side, a panther's fierce array, Diving, catching, saving every day. The slip cordon, waiting for the edge, A net of hands upon the grassy ledge.

IV. The Modern Tempo (Limited Overs)
The canvas changes, colours now alight, Beneath the stadium's powerful floodlight. The T20 smash, a whirlwind, sudden craze, Where strategy bows to explosive phase. The scoop, the ramp, the switches and the might, A dazzling spectacle in darkest night. The boundaries shrink, the sixes multiply, A frantic race beneath a hurried sky. The One-Day game, the balance held in grace, Of steady building and the final pace. The Death Overs arrive, the tension swells, Where every wide ball rings the final bells. The captains scheme, the fielders shift their stance, A high-stakes gamble, a breathless, urgent dance.

V. The Spirit of the Game
From village green to colosseum vast, This sport of elegance is built to last. It teaches patience, honour, and control, The silent battles that define the soul. A handshake shared, regardless of the score, The spirit of the game forevermore. It binds the world, across the ocean wide, Where rival nations stand with equal pride. So raise the bat, and polish up the ball, The game of cricket answers freedom's call. May the green pitch endure beneath the sun, Until the final, glorious run is done.
